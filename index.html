-RHS- Conversations In preparation

A dual-engine text-to-speech studio with voice design, script casting, phonetics, and transcription. One purchase, and it is yours.

What your purchase covers

  • A lifetime licence for the version you buy. No subscription, no account, no online check. It keeps working offline.
  • Free updates for every release in your major version. A big new version may be a discounted upgrade.
  • Real help. Email and Discord support from a person, plus free help pages.
  • A refund window if it is not right for you.
  • Use what you make. Streams, videos, games — as long as the voice you chose allows it. We tell you each voice's terms.

What it does not cover

  • Other people's licences and online services, such as third-party voices or cloud AI accounts.
  • Separate products, custom work, and free pre-release test builds.

Launch terms. Support length, refund period and number of PCs are confirmed at release.
